## Cobblemon

[Cobblemon](https://gitlab.com/cable-mc/cobblemon) is a Pokémon mod for Minecraft. PokeBrawl uses,
adapted, its:

- **Pokémon models, animations and textures**, licensed **CC BY-NC 3.0**
  ([game/pokemon/LICENSE-models.txt](game/pokemon/LICENSE-models.txt)). They are converted from
  Bedrock format by `tools/convert_cobblemon.py`; the untouched originals are in
  `sources/cobblemon/`. Used with the Cobblemon team's approval, non-commercially and with
  attribution.
- **Move particle pieces and sequences** (`game/fx/`), converted by `tools/convert_particles.py`,
  **move sounds and cries** (`game/sounds/`, `game/pokemon/*/cry.ogg`) and **battle GUI pieces**
  (`game/gui/cobblemon/`), under the repository's general license, **MPL 2.0**.
- **Species data** (base stats, types, abilities, learnsets) in
  `game/scripts/data/species.gd`, generated by `tools/import_species.py`, MPL 2.0.

Particle textures that Cobblemon copies from Minecraft (Mojang's) are not used: our own pixel
art versions with the same sizes are drawn by `tools/convert_particles.py`.

## Pokémon Showdown

[Pokémon Showdown](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown) (MIT) is the reference for how
moves and abilities behave. Its data is read as text; its code is not run or shipped.

## Our own

- The code (MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
- The stages, drawn by script (`game/scripts/arena.gd`, `game/scripts/glade_arena.gd`).
- The pixel font (`game/gui/font/`, `tools/pixel_font.py`).
- The pixel energy balls (`game/fx/tex/custom/`, `tools/move_sprites.py`).
- The synthesized placeholder sounds (dodge, footsteps, falls, armor, charges) in
  `game/scripts/audio.gd`.
