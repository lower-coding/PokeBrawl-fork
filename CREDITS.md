# Credits

PokeBrawl is made by [xjoje6](https://github.com/xjoje6). It stands on other people's work:

## Cobblemon

[Cobblemon](https://gitlab.com/cable-mc/cobblemon) is a Pokémon mod for Minecraft, by the
Cobblemon team. PokeBrawl uses, adapted, its:

- **Pokémon models, animations and textures** (the ten Pokémon, and Pikachu's cap variant for
  Ash's Pikachu), licensed **CC BY-NC 3.0**
  ([game/pokemon/LICENSE-models.txt](game/pokemon/LICENSE-models.txt),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/). They are converted from Bedrock format by
  `tools/convert_cobblemon.py` and changed for this game (new formats, poses and effects on
  top); the untouched originals are in `sources/cobblemon/`. Used with the Cobblemon team's
  approval, non-commercially and with attribution.
- **Move particle pieces, sequences and textures** (`game/fx/`, except `game/fx/tex/custom/`),
  converted by `tools/convert_particles.py`, under the repository's general license,
  **MPL 2.0**.
- **Sounds** (`game/sounds/`): move sounds, menu and interface sounds, Poké Ball sounds (throw,
  open, send out) and the Pokémon cries (`game/pokemon/*/cry.ogg`), MPL 2.0.
- **Battle GUI pieces** (`game/gui/cobblemon/`): type icons, status tabs and frames, MPL 2.0.
- **Species data** (base stats, types, abilities, weights, learnsets) in
  `game/scripts/data/species.gd`, generated by `tools/import_species.py`, MPL 2.0.

Particle textures that Cobblemon copies from Minecraft (Mojang's) are not used: our own pixel
art versions with the same sizes are drawn by `tools/convert_particles.py`.

## Pokémon Showdown

- [Pokémon Showdown](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown) (MIT) is the reference for how
  moves and abilities behave (power, accuracy, effects, flags). Its data is read as text; its
  code is not run or shipped.
- The **trainer sprites** (`game/trainers/`: Red, Ash, Lucas, Dawn, Ethan, Lyra, Hilbert, Hilda
  and Cynthia) are the ones Showdown serves at
  https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/. These nine carry no fan artist's credit
  there: they are the games' own sprites, which belong to Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and
  GAME FREAK. They are used unedited.
